# Story — Tutorial Investigation
## Detective Files — Case 00

- **Case ID:** `case-00`
- **Case Title:** Tutorial Investigation
- **Difficulty:** Tutorial
- **Estimated Playtime:** 10-15 minutes

---

## Premise

A small, deliberately simple incident designed to teach the full CID OS
investigation workflow, not to challenge the player's reasoning.

## Setting

A quiet street-corner bookstore, "Ellery & Finch Books," on a slow
weeknight.

## Incident

The bookstore's till was found short of cash the morning after closing,
and a small locked display case containing a signed first-edition book
was found broken into. The owner, Nora Finch, reports the loss and asks
CID to look into it before involving the store's insurer.

## Victim

**Nora Finch**, 45, bookstore owner. Present only as the reporting party
— not physically harmed, this is a property crime.

## Primary Suspect

**Callum Voss**, 26, a part-time evening clerk who closed the store alone
that night. Financial records show he was behind on rent. No prior
record.

## Witness

**Priya Shah**, 31, owner of the café next door, was closing up around
the same time and saw Callum still inside after the posted closing time,
alone, with the front lights off.

## Important Location

**Ellery & Finch Books**, the store itself — both the crime scene and
the only location the case requires the player to visit.

## Initial Evidence

- A short incident report from Nora Finch (the case brief).
- The broken display case latch (physical evidence).
- A crumpled receipt from a pawn shop found in the stockroom trash
  (document evidence — the key clue).

## Hidden Information

The pawn shop receipt is illegible at a glance; only after the player
requests analysis on it does the shop's location and a partial item
description become clear enough to confirm the stolen book was pawned
there under Callum's name.

## Investigation Timeline

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| ~21:00 | Priya Shah sees Callum still inside, alone, lights off. |
| ~21:00-21:30 | Callum breaks the display case, takes the book and till cash. |
| Next morning | Nora Finch discovers the loss and files the report. |

## Truth

Callum Voss took the cash and the signed first edition after closing,
alone, to cover overdue rent, and pawned the book the same night. This
is the case's one and only conclusion — no red herrings, no alternate
suspects.

## Resolution

Once the player connects the evidence, Priya's account, and the pawn
receipt to Callum Voss and submits their resolution, Headquarters
confirms the case is solved. The tutorial's job is done the moment the
player has done this once — see `design.md` for how replay resets this
state for a full re-teach on the next playthrough.
